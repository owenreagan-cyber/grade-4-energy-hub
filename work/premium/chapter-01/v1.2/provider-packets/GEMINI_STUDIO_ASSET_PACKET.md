> Provider: Gemini Studio (independent visual exploration).

## Role and limits

- Gemini receives **locked science briefs** and proposes visual directions / candidate concepts for review.
- Gemini does **not** invent wording or science; any generated text is treated as a draft to check against locked text, never as source truth.
- Gemini output is a **concept exploration** only; nothing it produces becomes `APPROVED` without `SCIENCE_REVIEW = PASS` and `ART_REVIEW = PASS`.

## Exploration targets (concept directions only)

1. **Chapter opener** — cinematic Discovery/Evidence treatment ideas (before/after, layered glyph).
2. **Vocabulary card compositions** — how to arrange TERM / hero visual / definition / footer for ENERGY, CHANGE, STORED ENERGY.
3. **Five-forms system** — one coherent visual family for motion · sound · light · heat · electrical energy.
4. **ENERGY → CHANGE → EVIDENCE** — node/arrow model layouts and reveal handling.
5. **Dark → bright treatment** — before/after framing ideas.
6. **Stored-energy treatments** — battery, rubber band, raised apple/object; STILL → LATER → CHANGE cue.

## Locked science briefs (hand to Gemini verbatim)

### Energy (Reader p. 1)
- energy = the ability to cause change
- change = to become different
- energy can be invisible; we look for the change

### Stored energy (Reader p. 4)
- stored energy = energy that has the ability to cause change at a later time
- a still object can store energy; position can relate to stored energy

### Five forms (Reader p. 3)
- motion · sound · light · heat · electrical energy
- each form can cause a change

### Model
- ENERGY → CHANGE → EVIDENCE (change is evidence energy was involved)

### Forbidden terminology (never in output)
kinetic · potential · gravitational potential · mechanical · radiant · compression wave · inertia

### Forbidden imagery
- energy drawn as a visible beam/substance/glow inside objects
- heat claimed where it wasn't observed
- mechanism-level physics (electrons, waves, currents)
- copyrighted trading-card imitation (no Pokémon branding, no rarity gems, no card-back, no game mechanics)

## Deliverable format (for each direction)

- A small set of candidate concepts (thumbnails or descriptions), each tagged with the target asset `VISUAL_ID`.
- No locked text changes. If Gemini suggests wording, it must be flagged as DRAFT and diffed against locked text.

## Disposition

Candidate concepts go to Chief visual review; only Chief-selected directions advance to production briefs and generation. Gemini is a proposal source, not a sign-off.
