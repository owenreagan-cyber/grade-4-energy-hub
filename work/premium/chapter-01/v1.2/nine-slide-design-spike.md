# CHAPTER 1 PREMIUM v1.2 — NINE-SLIDE DESIGN SPIKE

> Detailed briefs for nine representative slides. These define the visual target and the acceptance bar before any mass artwork is produced. No final artwork is generated in this phase.

---

## SPIKE 1 — Chapter opener

- **SLIDE:** 1
- **SLIDE_CLASS:** CINEMATIC_CONCEPT
- **PURPOSE:** Open the chapter; establish the Discovery/Evidence theme; preview the central model (ENERGY → CHANGE → ?).
- **CURRENT_PROBLEM:** Flat programmatic title; no imagery; the "?" reads as decoration, not a mystery.
- **MUST_COMMUNICATE:** "Energy Causes Change" is an investigation — a change is the clue that energy was involved.
- **STUDENT_ACTION:** Look and listen (whole-class framing; no writing).
- **TEXT_THAT_MUST_REMAIN:** `CHAPTER 1 · GRADE 4 · WEEK 1` · `Energy Causes Change` · `Discovery / Evidence` · `Unit 1 · Energy Transfer & Transformation` · BQ teaser `Where can we observe evidence of energy causing change?`
- **NOTES_ANCHOR:** — (title; exact BQ lives on slide 2)
- **VISUAL_BRIEF:** A warm, cinematic hero: a real before/after scene (e.g., a dark room becoming lit, or a pencil tip before/after) with a subtle ENERGY → CHANGE → ? glyph, navy/gold/cream.
- **PREFERRED_MODALITY:** Photography with restrained graphic overlay.
- **FORBIDDEN_OR_MISLEADING_ELEMENTS:** No answer in the "?" (EVIDENCE must not be revealed here); no childlike clip art; no corporate-template look.
- **ASPECT_PLACEMENT_NEEDS:** 16:9; keep 40 pt safe inset for projector overscan; title clear at projector distance.
- **ART_PRIORITY:** REQUIRED
- **REQUIRED/PREFERRED/OPTIONAL:** REQUIRED

## SPIKE 2 — Dark room → bright room

- **SLIDE:** 4
- **SLIDE_CLASS:** CINEMATIC_CONCEPT (phenomenon)
- **PURPOSE:** First observable change (Reader p. 1) — the room becoming different.
- **CURRENT_PROBLEM:** Text-only DARK/BRIGHT panels; no real evidence image.
- **MUST_COMMUNICATE:** "Something became different. That is a change." — the room went from hard-to-see to light-filled.
- **STUDENT_ACTION:** Observe (OBSERVE HUD).
- **TEXT_THAT_MUST_REMAIN:** `What changed?` · `Reader p. 1 — you walk into a dark room and flip a switch.` · `DARK room · hard to see` · `BRIGHT room · light fills the room` · `Something became different. That is a change.`
- **NOTES_ANCHOR:** — (concept; feeds BQ)
- **VISUAL_BRIEF:** Identical-framing before/after: a dim room vs. the same room lit. The *change* is the only variable.
- **PREFERRED_MODALITY:** Real photograph, before/after pair.
- **FORBIDDEN_OR_MISLEADING_ELEMENTS:** No "energy" arrow drawn as a visible beam; no suggestion energy is a visible substance.
- **ASPECT_PLACEMENT_NEEDS:** Two equal side-by-side panels (BEFORE / AFTER), grayscale-safe.
- **ART_PRIORITY:** REQUIRED
- **REQUIRED/PREFERRED/OPTIONAL:** REQUIRED

## SPIKE 3 — Pencil sharpener BEFORE/AFTER

- **SLIDE:** 8 (part of the 6–9 demo arc)
- **SLIDE_CLASS:** CINEMATIC_CONCEPT (demonstration reveal)
- **PURPOSE:** Show the live-demo result — the observable changes that are evidence of energy.
- **CURRENT_PROBLEM:** Text-only BEFORE/AFTER; the evidence is described, not shown.
- **MUST_COMMUNICATE:** The tip got sharper, the pencil got shorter, it made a sound — those changes are the evidence.
- **STUDENT_ACTION:** Observe (OBSERVE HUD); then discuss what changed.
- **TEXT_THAT_MUST_REMAIN:** `What changed?` · `BEFORE · dull tip · quiet` · `AFTER · shorter · sharper · humming` · `CHANGES · the tip is sharper · the pencil is shorter · it makes a sound`
- **NOTES_ANCHOR:** — (demo; feeds N1/BQ evidence framing)
- **VISUAL_BRIEF:** Real before/after photo pair of the same pencil (dull vs. sharpened tip) plus the hum/sound cue; identical framing and crop.
- **PREFERRED_MODALITY:** Real photograph, before/after pair.
- **FORBIDDEN_OR_MISLEADING_ELEMENTS:** No false "energy visible inside the sharpener"; no heat claim (unobserved).
- **ASPECT_PLACEMENT_NEEDS:** Two equal panels + a CHANGES list; keep large labels.
- **ART_PRIORITY:** REQUIRED
- **REQUIRED/PREFERRED/OPTIONAL:** REQUIRED

## SPIKE 7 — Five Forms of Energy visual

- **SLIDE:** 17
- **SLIDE_CLASS:** CINEMATIC_CONCEPT (concept)
- **PURPOSE:** Introduce five forms — motion · sound · light · heat · electrical energy (Reader p. 3).
- **CURRENT_PROBLEM:** Only motion is modeled (carousel); four forms are bare text.
- **MUST_COMMUNICATE:** Each form is one kind of energy that can cause a change.
- **STUDENT_ACTION:** Identify the forms (TRACE/RECITE); find other forms in the Reader.
- **TEXT_THAT_MUST_REMAIN:** `Energy has different forms` · `Reader p. 3 — think of a spinning carousel.` · `motion` · `sound` · `light` · `heat` · `electrical energy` · `On the carousel, the horses spin and move up and down. That is motion. Find the other forms in the Reader.`
- **NOTES_ANCHOR:** N2
- **VISUAL_BRIEF:** A five-card row (or fan): one clear, concrete example per form, each with a change cue (motion = spinning horses; sound = a bell; light = a lamp; heat = warm cocoa; electrical = a lit circuit/bulb). Consistent card architecture.
- **PREFERRED_MODALITY:** Illustration/photography per form, unified card row.
- **FORBIDDEN_OR_MISLEADING_ELEMENTS:** No "electricity is a form of energy flowing like water" mechanical detail; no compression-wave language; no heat-as-fire confusion.
- **ASPECT_PLACEMENT_NEEDS:** Five equal cards across 16:9; grayscale-safe; large labels.
- **ART_PRIORITY:** REQUIRED
- **REQUIRED/PREFERRED/OPTIONAL:** REQUIRED

## SPIKE 9 — Representative WRITE/CHECK slide (visual restraint)

- **SLIDE:** 11 (V1 write) — representative of all 20 WRITE_CHECK slides (11/12, 15/16, 19, 21/22, 26/27, 29, 30/31, 33, answer slides 35/37/39/41/43/45).
- **SLIDE_CLASS:** WRITE_CHECK
- **PURPOSE:** Students write the missing word(s) into Guided Notes; the projected slide mirrors the notes exactly.
- **CURRENT_PROBLEM:** None functionally — this is the restraint benchmark. Any redesign risk is over-decoration.
- **MUST_COMMUNICATE:** The exact notes anchor (V1), the blank, and the write cue.
- **STUDENT_ACTION:** WRITE into Guided Notes p. 1.
- **TEXT_THAT_MUST_REMAIN:** `Vocabulary — Energy & Change` · `Guided Notes · Reader p. 1` · `energy — the ability to cause _______ .` · `change — to become _________ .` · `Write the missing word in each blank.`
- **NOTES_ANCHOR:** V1
- **VISUAL_BRIEF:** **No change.** Keep the clean notes mirror: primary section label, ruled blanks, WRITE HUD. The only permissible polish is reusing the premium vocabulary card frame at reduced size if it does not crowd the writing surface.
- **PREFERRED_MODALITY:** Flat notes mirror (text + rules); no photography, no illustration.
- **FORBIDDEN_OR_MISLEADING_ELEMENTS:** No answer visible on a WRITE slide; no decoration that shrinks writing space; no reveal-before-write.
- **ASPECT_PLACEMENT_NEEDS:** Same layout as the matching notes page for easy transfer.
- **ART_PRIORITY:** NONE
- **REQUIRED/PREFERRED/OPTIONAL:** NONE (restraint is the acceptance criterion)
