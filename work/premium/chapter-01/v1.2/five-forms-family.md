# CHAPTER 1 PREMIUM v1.2 — FIVE-FORMS VISUAL FAMILY

> The five forms — motion · sound · light · heat · electrical energy — are ONE coherent visual family (slide 17, anchor N2).

## Family requirements

1. **One coherent system.** The five cards must look intentionally related — one shared frame, one palette, one icon/label language, one composition grid. Five unrelated styles = REJECTED.
2. **Exact labels.** Science labels are the accepted terminology only, verbatim:
   - `motion`
   - `sound`
   - `light`
   - `heat`
   - `electrical energy`
3. **No advanced forms vocabulary.** No kinetic/potential/radiant/compression-wave/mechanical/inertia. "electrical energy" is the exact Reader term — do not shorten to "electricity" and do not expand to "electric current".

## Per-card anatomy

| Region | Content |
|---|---|
| Term label | the exact form word (large, `primary`) |
| Hero visual | ONE concrete example, showing a change cue |
| Change cue | a short hint of what changes (visual, not a new claim) |

## Concrete examples (locked for spike)

| Form | Example | Change cue |
|---|---|---|
| motion | spinning carousel horses | horses move up/down |
| sound | a ringing bell | it makes a sound |
| light | a lamp lighting a space | the room gets brighter |
| heat | warm cocoa / radiator | it feels warmer |
| electrical energy | a lit bulb or device | it turns on |

## Family palette / style

- Chapter 1 theme: navy `#1F2A5A` primary · gold `#A67C16` accent · cream `#F5EEDC` surface · amber `#7A5620` neutral.
- Grayscale-safe: color never the only signal; label text + shape + position always present.
- One card grid: five equal cards across 16:9, large labels (≥ 28 pt).

## Modality decision (Chief decision, Phase 2B — LOCKED)

**REALISTIC INSTRUCTIONAL ILLUSTRATION.** Use recognizable real-world objects/events with a coherent premium illustration style.

Do **NOT** use:
- abstract energy beams
- glowing energy matter
- particle streams
- fake electron visualization
- unrelated decorative symbols
- cartoon clip art

The five-form family must be visually coherent as ONE set.

## Science constraints

- No "electricity flows like water" mechanism.
- No compression-wave detail for sound.
- No fire = heat confusion; heat is shown as temperature change (warming).
- Each form shows a **change** (energy causes change), not an abstract "energy field".

## Locked per-form depiction (Chief decision, Phase 2B)

| Form | Preferred depiction |
|---|---|
| motion | recognizable moving object / carousel / ball; observable motion, **not** abstract streak effects as the science |
| sound | recognizable sound-producing object; subtle physical vibration cue allowed; do **not** imply visible sound rays are literal matter |
| light | real luminous source visibly illuminating an environment/object |
| heat | real warm/hot object with plausible observable context; avoid unnecessary flame imagery |
| electrical energy | powered real-world device/source producing an observable change; do **not** show speculative electron mechanics |

No advanced terminology.
