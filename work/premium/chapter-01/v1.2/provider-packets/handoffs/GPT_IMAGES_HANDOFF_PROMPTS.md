# GPT IMAGES — GENERATION HANDOFF PROMPTS (Chapter 1 Premium v1.2)

> Clean copy/paste prompts. Each is self-contained: it locks the science, composition, forbidden implications, and style, and leaves room for visual creativity. **Do not reveal prior candidate rankings, the expected winner, or any audit history.**

## Global rules for every prompt below

- Grade 4 elementary science. Premium modern, warm, academically credible, cinematic. Slight dimensionality. **Not** childish clip art, **not** generic corporate template, **not** busy stock.
- Palette: deep navy `#1F2A5A`, warm gold `#A67C16`, cream `#F5EEDC`, muted amber `#7A5620`. Color is never the only signal (must survive grayscale).
- Forbidden imagery (every asset): energy drawn as a visible beam/substance/glow inside objects; particle streams; fake electron visuals; mechanism-level physics.
- Forbidden terminology (every asset, do not render any words containing): kinetic, potential, mechanical, radiant, compression wave, inertia.
- Do **not** render instructional text into the artwork except where a prompt explicitly says a label is overlaid later (in those cases, leave a clean region).

## 1. CH1-HERO-01 — Chapter opener (1 prompt → 2–3 candidates)

```
A premium elementary-science chapter opener illustration, 16:9.
Theme: DISCOVERY / EVIDENCE. A child-like sense of "a change is the clue
that energy was involved."
Show ONE warm, cinematic before/after scene: the same cozy classroom or
bedroom first dim and quiet, then bright and lively after a switch is
flipped. The room itself is the subject; the change (dark -> bright) must
be unmistakable.
Subtle graphic overlay: three rounded tokens in a left-to-right row reading
as ENERGY -> CHANGE -> ? — but render the tokens as simple shapes with NO
text inside (the words are added later). The third token must be a "?"
shape, NOT the word EVIDENCE, and must feel unresolved/mysterious.
Palette: deep navy, warm gold, cream, muted amber. Warm, cinematic,
slightly dimensional, high contrast. Grayscale-safe.
Leave a clean, low-contrast zone across the top or lower third for a title
overlay.
DO NOT: draw energy as a visible beam or glow; reveal any answer in the
third token; use clip art or a corporate template.
```

## 6. CH1-FORMS family — five forms (1 prompt → 2–3 candidates of the FULL family)

```
A COHERENT set of five premium instructional illustrations for the five
forms of energy, Grade 4. They must look like ONE intentional family: same
style, same palette, same card grid, same level of detail. Landscape 16:9,
five equal cards across.

Card 1 - motion: a recognizable moving object (spinning carousel horses or
a rolling ball); show observable motion, NOT abstract streak effects as
the science.
Card 2 - sound: a recognizable sound-producing object (a ringing hand
bell); a subtle physical vibration cue is allowed; do NOT draw sound as
visible rays of matter.
Card 3 - light: a real luminous source (a lamp) visibly illuminating a
room or object.
Card 4 - heat: a real warm object (a mug of hot cocoa with gentle steam)
with a plausible "it warms things" context; avoid flames.
Card 5 - electrical energy: a powered real-world device (a small lamp or
toy turning on) producing an observable change; do NOT show speculative
electron mechanics.

Each card: one concrete example + a subtle change cue, with a clean empty
band at the top for a label (the words motion / sound / light / heat /
electrical energy are added later). No text anywhere in the artwork.
Premium, warm, cinematic, slightly dimensional. Palette: deep navy, warm
gold, cream, muted amber. Grayscale-safe.
DO NOT: abstract energy beams, glowing energy matter, particle streams,
fake electron visuals, unrelated decorative symbols, cartoon clip art,
unrelated five styles.
```
