# PHOTO / REALITY — GENERATION REQUIREMENTS (Chapter 1 Premium v1.2)

> Realistic photography / photorealistic treatment for observable physical evidence. These are **acquisition/shoot requirements** (real photography preferred; photorealistic rendering only where photography is impractical).
> **Do not substitute generic stock or icons.** A missing asset remains `CANDIDATE_PENDING`.

## Global requirements

- Real, clean, high-contrast, no busy stock-photo backgrounds.
- Grade 4: the changed variable must be visually obvious at projector distance.
- Palette-friendly (navy/gold/cream/amber context where a backdrop exists); grayscale-safe.
- No text in the photo (labels are overlaid deterministically later).

## Assets & requirements

### 1. CH1-DARK-BRIGHT-01 — dark → bright room
- Two equal side-by-side panels; **identical framing and crop**; the change is the only variable.
- Real photograph preferred. Warm, no busy background. No visible energy beam.

### 2. CH1-PENCIL-BEFORE-01 — dull pencil BEFORE
- Single subject (dull pencil tip + electric sharpener), 50–65% of frame.
- Must read "dull / unsharpened". No post-sharpening state.

### 3. CH1-PENCIL-AFTER-01 — sharpened pencil AFTER
- **Identical framing + crop to BEFORE** (same subject, same angle, same lighting).
- Shorter + sharper must be obvious when paired. No "hum" drawn; no unobserved heat.

### 4. CH1-STORED-BATTERY-01 — battery in flashlight
- Battery visible inside a flashlight that is clearly **OFF** (still, stored).
- No visible energy inside; no lit state.

### 5. CH1-STORED-RUBBER-BAND-01 — stretched rubber band
- Band **stretched but NOT released** (held between fingers).
- No release outcome in the same image.

### 6. CH1-STORED-RAISED-APPLE-01 — raised object (position)
- Apple held above the ground with a clear ground reference; obvious height difference.
- Show position, not motion; no "energy inside the apple".

## Matched-framing rule (hard)

For every before/after pair, everything identical except the changed variable: same subject, angle, crop, lighting. Avoid busy backgrounds.

## Candidate count

2–3 candidate shots per asset (or per before/after pair). Purpose is visual-direction choice, not exhaustive search.
